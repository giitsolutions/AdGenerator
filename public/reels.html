About the video source

Only new videos focused on a treadmill (in use or as equipment, any setting) are accepted. Clips where a treadmill is only in the background and unrelated exercise footage are rejected. Stock clips are never reused. For a slideshow, choose treadmill-related photos from your library.

About uploads

Use your own MP4 (up to 90 seconds by default). Campaign text and the logo are added, and a caption and hashtags are written. Common failures: wrong or corrupt MP4, file too large or long, missing campaign, or video rendering unavailable.
