✨ Generate a video

Automatic and stock modes accept only new videos focused on a treadmill, in use or as equipment, in any setting. Walking, jogging, running, incline, interval, and other treadmill videos are supported. Clips where a treadmill is only in the background and unrelated exercise footage are rejected. Stock clips are never reused. For a slideshow, choose treadmill-related photos from your library.

⬆️ Upload — your own video

Use your own MP4 video (up to 90 seconds by default). We add campaign text and place the logo in a corner, then write its caption and hashtags. Common failures: wrong/corrupt MP4, file too large or long, missing campaign, or video rendering unavailable.
