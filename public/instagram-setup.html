Connect your Instagram

One click. Facebook opens, you approve, and you come straight back here. Connecting Instagram also turns on posting to your Facebook Page.

  1. Log in to Facebook with the account that is Admin of your Page.
  2. Tick your Instagram account, then your Facebook Page.
  3. Leave every permission switched on and press Continue or Save.
  4. You land back here and this card turns green.

First time, or Facebook shows an error? Follow the checklist below. It takes about 10 minutes once.
