Connect Instagram to KwikPost

Follow these steps once. After that you can approve posts and publish from the app.

1

Use a Business or Creator account

Personal Instagram accounts cannot publish via the API.

  • Open the Instagram app → Profile → Menu → Settings and activity
  • Go to Account type and tools
  • Switch to Professional → choose Business or Creator
You keep the same username and posts. Only the account type changes.
2

Create or link a Facebook Page

Instagram publishing needs a Facebook Page linked to that Instagram account.

  • Go to facebook.com/pages/create
  • Create a Page for your brand (or use an existing one)
  • On Instagram: Settings → Account → Linked accounts / Sharing to other apps → Facebook → link this Page
  • Or from Facebook Page settings → Linked accounts → Instagram → connect
The Facebook user who clicks Connect in KwikPost must be an admin of that Page.

Connect inside KwikPost

Stay logged into KwikPost, then start Facebook Login. Allow the permissions when asked.

  • Manage your Page
  • Access Instagram account
  • Publish content to Instagram

Checking connection…
After success you return to the app with Instagram connected. You only need this again if you disconnect or change accounts.
5

Publish your first post

  • Open Posts or Reels
  • Generate or upload content
  • Press Approve (or schedule)
If Approve asks you to connect Instagram, finish step 4 first, then approve again.

Common problems

  • No Pages found — Page not linked, or you used a different Facebook login.
  • No Instagram business account — Link IG ↔ Page again; wait a few minutes and retry.
  • Permission denied — Accept all requested permissions on the Facebook screen.
  • App in development mode — Only Facebook app testers/admins can connect until the app is Live (developer setup).
