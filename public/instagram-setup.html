Connect your Instagram in 5 steps

Do one step at a time. Each step has the links you need. The first time takes about 15 minutes.

Before you start, you need:
  • An Instagram account (it can be a new one)
  • A Facebook account, logged in on this browser
  • A Facebook Page that you admin (step 2 shows how to make one)
Why Facebook? Instagram publishing goes through a Facebook Page. Your Instagram account must be linked to that Page.
0 of 5 steps done
1

Switch Instagram to a Business or Creator account

Personal accounts cannot publish through the API. Switching does not delete posts or change your username.

  1. Open the Instagram app on your phone (or click the button below to open Instagram in the browser).
  2. Go to your profile, tap ☰ Menu, then Settings and activity.
  3. Tap Account type and tools, then Switch to professional account.
  4. Choose Business or Creator, pick a category (for example Gym or Fitness), and tap Done.
Open Instagram Instagram help: business account
Check: your profile now shows a Professional dashboard or Insights option.
2

Create a Facebook Page (skip if you have one)

The Page is the business identity that Instagram publishes through.

  1. Click Create a Facebook Page below. It opens in a new tab.
  2. Enter the Page name (your business name) and a Category such as Gym or Fitness.
  3. Add a short bio and profile picture if you want, then click Create Page.
  4. To check your Pages later, click See my Pages. Make sure you see the Page with an Admin label.
Create a Facebook Page See my Pages Log in to Facebook
You must be an Admin of this Page. If you only see it as an editor or moderator, ask the Page owner to make you Admin in Page settings, then continue.
3

Link Instagram to that Page

This tells Facebook that the Instagram account belongs to your Page. You can do it from Facebook or from Instagram.

Option A (recommended): from Accounts Center

  1. Click Open Accounts Center below.
  2. Click Add accounts (or Accounts, then Add account) and choose Instagram.
  3. Log in to the Instagram Business or Creator account and confirm.
  4. Check that the Instagram account and your Facebook Page appear in the same Accounts Center.

Option B: from Instagram

  1. In Instagram, go to Settings and activity, then Accounts Center.
  2. Tap Add Facebook Page (or Connected experiences), choose your Page, and confirm.
Open Accounts Center Facebook help: link Instagram to a Page Instagram: Accounts Center
Check: in Accounts Center, your Instagram account is listed next to your Facebook Page.
4

Connect Instagram to KwikPost

This button opens Facebook. Log in with the account that is Admin of your Page, then approve the permissions.

  1. Make sure the button below is enabled. It turns on after steps 1 to 3 are ticked.
  2. Click Connect Instagram. Facebook opens.
  3. Choose the Facebook Page and the Instagram account when asked.
  4. Allow every permission (Page access, Instagram account, content publishing), then click Continue.
  5. You come back to this page automatically.
If Facebook shows "App not active", see the box for the app owner at the bottom of this page.
5

Check that it works

When the connection is saved, this step turns green automatically. You can then post reels and posts to Instagram.

For the app owner only: adding a new user

The Meta app must allow each new Facebook account. While the app is unpublished, only app roles can log in.

  1. Open Meta for Developers, My Apps and select the KwikPost app.
  2. In the left menu, open App roles, then Roles.
  3. Click Add people, type the new user's Facebook name, choose Administrator or Developer, and click Add.
  4. The new user accepts the invitation at developers.facebook.com/settings/roles.

Test users cannot be added as roles. Use a real Facebook account.
