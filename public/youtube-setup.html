Connect your YouTube in 5 steps

Your reels are uploaded to your channel as YouTube Shorts. Each step has the links you need. It takes about 10 minutes.

Before you start, you need:
  • A Google (Gmail) account that owns the YouTube channel
  • A YouTube channel on that account (step 1 shows how)
  • The app owner to add your Gmail as a tester (step 2). This is needed while the app is in testing.
0 of 5 steps done
1

Make sure you have a YouTube channel

The channel must exist on the Google account you will connect.

  1. Click Open YouTube below and sign in with your Gmail.
  2. Click your profile picture (top right). If you see Create a channel, click it and follow the steps.
  3. Choose a channel name and handle, then click Create channel.
  4. Your channel page should open. You are done with this step.
Open YouTube Create a channel YouTube help: create a channel
2

Contact the owner for approval

Google only lets approved Gmail accounts connect while the app is in testing. Contact the owner directly:

  1. Type the Gmail address that owns your YouTube channel.
  2. Tap WhatsApp the owner or Email the owner. The message is already written.
  3. Send it. The owner adds your Gmail under Test users and replies.
  4. When the owner confirms, tick the box below.
WhatsApp the owner Email the owner Owner: open Audience page
Owner contact:
Without this approval, Google shows "Access blocked" when you try to connect.
3

Sign in to the right Gmail in this browser

Google must use the same Gmail that the owner added in step 2.

  1. Look at the profile picture at the top right of this browser.
  2. If it shows a different account, click it and choose Add account or Switch account.
  3. Sign in with the correct Gmail. Check it at My Account below.
Google sign-in My Google Account (check)
4

Connect YouTube to KwikPost

This button opens Google's permission screen. Allow both YouTube permissions.

  1. Make sure the button below is enabled. It turns on after steps 1 to 3 are ticked.
  2. Click Connect YouTube. Google opens.
  3. Choose your Gmail. If you see "Google hasn't verified this app", click Continue.
  4. Tick both boxes: "Manage your YouTube videos" and "View your YouTube account". Missing one causes uploads to fail.
  5. Click Continue. You return to this page automatically.
Tip: if the boxes are not ticked, click Select all first, then Continue.
5

Check that it works

When YouTube is connected, this step turns green automatically. Then you can upload reels as Shorts.

  1. Go to Reels, open a draft, and press Post or Schedule.
  2. Choose YouTube Shorts only for a test.
  3. When done, check your channel in YouTube Studio.

Something went wrong? (common problems and fixes)
What you seeWhat to do
"Access blocked" or "access_denied"Your Gmail is not a test user. Ask the owner to add it (step 2), then try again.
"Google hasn't verified this app"Expected while testing. Click Continue (or Advanced, then Go to the app).
"redirect_uri_mismatch"The owner's Google Cloud redirect address does not match the one below. Send the owner the exact address from the owner section.
"Approval email was not sent"Copy the red message under step 2 and send it to the app owner or the developer.
Upload fails after connectingDisconnect, then connect again and tick both permission boxes.
Wrong account openedSign out of Google in this browser, sign in with the right Gmail, then press Connect again.
Connect button stays offTick steps 1, 2 and 3 first. Then reload with Ctrl + Shift + R.
Old connection causes errorsOpen Google permissions, remove KwikPost, then connect again.
For the app owner only: adding a tester and checking settings

Add a tester Gmail:

  1. Open Google Auth Platform, Audience.
  2. Under Test users, click + Add users, enter the Gmail, and click Save.

Check the app settings:

  1. YouTube Data API v3 must show Enabled.
  2. Clients: the web client must list the redirect address below under Authorized redirect URIs.
  3. Data Access (scopes): youtube.upload and youtube.readonly must be added.

Redirect address for this site:

Use the same address in YOUTUBE_REDIRECT_URI in the server's .env file.
