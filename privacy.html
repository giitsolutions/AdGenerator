KwikPost Privacy Policy

KwikPost helps businesses create and schedule posts and short videos for Instagram and YouTube.

Data we use

We use your Google account email and the YouTube access you grant, only to upload videos you choose to your YouTube channel. We store the access token securely on our server.

Data we do not sell

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except as needed to upload content to the platforms you connect.

Revoking access

You can remove KwikPost at any time from Google permissions.
